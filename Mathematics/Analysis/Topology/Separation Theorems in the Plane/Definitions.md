# Definitions.md

> [!tip] 61.1 Arc
> An arc is a topological space that is homeomorphic to the closed unit interval $[0, 1]$.

> [!tip] 61.2 Simple Closed Curve
> A simple closed curve is a topological space that is homeomorphic to the unit circle $S^1$.

> [!tip] 61.3 Separation by a Set
> A subset $C$ of a connected topological space $X$ is said to **separate** $X$ if the subspace $X - C$ is not connected.

> [!tip] 63.1 Theta Space
> A theta space is a topological space that is a union of three arcs $A$, $B$, and $C$, each pair of which intersects precisely in their common end points. It is homeomorphic to the Greek letter $\Theta$.

> [!tip] 64.1 Graph, Vertices, and Edges
> A graph is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$ consisting of a finite number of points called **vertices**, and a finite number of arcs called **edges**, such that each edge intersects the set of vertices precisely in its end points, and the intersection of any two distinct edges is either empty or a single common vertex.

> [!tip] 64.2 Complete Graph ($K_n$)
> The complete graph on $n$ vertices, denoted $K_n$, is a graph containing exactly $n$ vertices, with exactly one edge joining each pair of distinct vertices.

> [!tip] 64.3 Bipartite Graph ($K_{m,n}$)
> The complete bipartite graph $K_{m,n}$ is a graph whose vertices are partitioned into two disjoint sets of sizes $m$ and $n$, with exactly one edge joining each vertex of the first set to each vertex of the second set, and no edges connecting vertices within the same set.

> [!tip] 64.4 Imbedding a Graph
> A graph is said to be **imbeddable in the plane** if it is homeomorphic to a graph that is a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^2$.
