# Theorems.md

> [!theorem] Theorem 39.1
> Let $\mathcal{A}$ be a locally finite collection of subsets of $X$. Then:
> 1. Any subcollection of $\mathcal{A}$ is locally finite.
> 2. The collection $\mathcal{B} = \{\overline{A} \mid A \in \mathcal{A}\}$ of the closures of the elements of $\mathcal{A}$ is locally finite.
> 3. $\overline{\bigcup_{A \in \mathcal{A}} A} = \bigcup_{A \in \mathcal{A}} \overline{A}$.

> [!theorem] Lemma 39.2
> Let $X$ be a metrizable space. If $\mathcal{A}$ is an open covering of $X$, then there is an open covering $\mathcal{E}$ of $X$ refining $\mathcal{A}$ that is countably locally finite.

> [!theorem] Theorem 40.3 (The Nagata-Smirnov Metrization Theorem)
> A space $X$ is metrizable if and only if $X$ is regular and has a countably locally finite basis.

> [!theorem] Theorem 41.1
> Every paracompact Hausdorff space is normal.

> [!theorem] Theorem 41.2
> Every closed subspace of a paracompact space is paracompact.

> [!theorem] Theorem 41.4 (A. H. Stone's Theorem)
> Every metrizable space is paracompact.

> [!theorem] Theorem 41.5
> Every regular Lindelöf space is paracompact.

> [!theorem] Theorem 42.1 (The Smirnov Metrization Theorem)
> A space $X$ is metrizable if and only if it is a paracompact Hausdorff space that is locally metrizable.
