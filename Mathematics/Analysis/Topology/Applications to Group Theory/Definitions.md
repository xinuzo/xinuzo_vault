# Definitions.md

> [!tip] 83.1 Graph (1-dimensional Complex)
> In this chapter, a **graph** $X$ is defined as a space that is the union of a set of points called vertices and a set of open arcs called edges, such that the intersection of any two distinct edges is empty, and the boundary of each edge consists of one or two vertices.

> [!tip] 83.2 Tree
> A **tree** is a connected graph that contains no simple closed curves. Equivalently, it is a graph that is simply connected ($\pi_1(T)$ is trivial).

> [!tip] 83.3 Maximal Tree
> A **maximal tree** in a connected graph $X$ is a subgraph that is a tree and that contains every vertex of $X$. (It acts as a simply connected "skeleton" for the graph).

> [!tip] 85.1 Rank of a Free Group
> The **rank** of a free group $F$ is the cardinality of its set of free generators. (Two free groups are isomorphic if and only if they have the same rank).

> [!tip] 85.2 Index of a Subgroup
> Let $H$ be a subgroup of a group $G$. The **index** of $H$ in $G$, denoted $[G : H]$, is the number of distinct right cosets (or left cosets) of $H$ in $G$.
