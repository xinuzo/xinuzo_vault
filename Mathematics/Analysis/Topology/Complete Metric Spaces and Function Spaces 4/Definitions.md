> [!tip] 43.1 Cauchy Sequence
> A sequence $(x_n)$ in a metric space $(X, d)$ is said to be a Cauchy sequence if for every $\epsilon > 0$, there is an integer $N$ such that $d(x_n, x_m) < \epsilon$ for all $n, m \ge N$.

> [!tip] 43.2 Complete Metric Space
> A metric space $(X, d)$ is said to be complete if every Cauchy sequence in $X$ converges to a point of $X$.

> [!tip] 43.3 Isometry and Isometric Embedding
> Let $(X, d_X)$ and $(Y, d_Y)$ be metric spaces. A function $f: X \to Y$ is an isometric embedding if $d_Y(f(x), f(x')) = d_X(x, x')$ for all $x, x' \in X$. If $f$ is also surjective, it is called an isometry, and the spaces are said to be isometric.

> [!tip] 45.1 Totally Bounded
> A metric space $(X, d)$ is said to be totally bounded if for every $\epsilon > 0$, there is a finite covering of $X$ by $\epsilon$-balls.

> [!tip] 46.1 Pointwise Convergence
> A sequence of functions $f_n: X \to Y$ converges pointwise to $f: X \to Y$ if for each $x \in X$, the sequence of points $(f_n(x))$ converges to $f(x)$ in the space $Y$.

> [!tip] 46.2 Uniform Convergence
> Let $Y$ be a metric space with metric $d$. A sequence of functions $f_n: X \to Y$ converges uniformly to $f: X \to Y$ if for every $\epsilon > 0$, there exists an integer $N$ such that $d(f_n(x), f(x)) < \epsilon$ for all $n \ge N$ and all $x \in X$.

> [!tip] 46.3 Compact Convergence
> A sequence of functions $f_n: X \to Y$ converges compactly to $f: X \to Y$ if for every compact subspace $C$ of $X$, the sequence of restricted functions $f_n|C$ converges uniformly to $f|C$.

> [!tip] 47.1 Compact-Open Topology
> Let $X$ and $Y$ be topological spaces. The compact-open topology on the set $C(X, Y)$ of all continuous functions from $X$ to $Y$ is the topology generated by the subbasis consisting of all sets of the form $S(C, U) = \{f \in C(X, Y) \mid f(C) \subset U\}$, where $C$ is a compact subspace of $X$ and $U$ is an open subset of $Y$.

> [!tip] 48.1 Baire Space
> A topological space $X$ is a Baire space if the following condition holds: Given any countable collection $\{A_n\}$ of closed sets of $X$, each of which has empty interior in $X$, their union $\bigcup A_n$ also has empty interior in $X$.
