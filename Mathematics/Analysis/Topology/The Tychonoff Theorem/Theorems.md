# Theorems.md

> [!theorem] Theorem 37.3 (The Tychonoff Theorem)
> An arbitrary product of compact spaces is compact in the product topology.

> [!theorem] Theorem 38.1 (Embedding Theorem)
> Let $X$ be a space. Then $X$ is completely regular if and only if it is homeomorphic to a subspace of a compact Hausdorff space.

> [!theorem] Theorem 38.2
> A space $X$ is completely regular if and only if there exists a compactification of $X$.

> [!theorem] Theorem 38.3 (The Stone-Čech Compactification / Extension Theorem)
> Let $X$ be a completely regular space. There exists a compactification $Y$ of $X$ having the property that every continuous map $f: X \to C$ of $X$ into a compact Hausdorff space $C$ extends uniquely to a continuous map $g: Y \to C$. 

> [!theorem] Theorem 38.4 (Uniqueness of the Stone-Čech Compactification)
> Let $X$ be a completely regular space. If $Y_1$ and $Y_2$ are two compactifications of $X$ that both satisfy the extension property of Theorem 38.3, then $Y_1$ and $Y_2$ are equivalent.
