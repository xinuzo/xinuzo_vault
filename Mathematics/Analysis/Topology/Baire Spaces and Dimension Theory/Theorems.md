# Theorems.md

> [!theorem] Theorem 48.1 (Equivalent Definition of a Baire Space)
> A space $X$ is a Baire space if and only if given any countable collection $\{U_n\}$ of open sets in $X$, each of which is dense in $X$, their intersection $\bigcap U_n$ is also dense in $X$.

> [!theorem] Theorem 48.2 (The Baire Category Theorem)
> If $X$ is a compact Hausdorff space or a complete metric space, then $X$ is a Baire space.

> [!theorem] Theorem 48.3
> Any open subspace of a Baire space is a Baire space.

> [!theorem] Theorem 48.4
> Any $G_\delta$ set in a locally compact Hausdorff space or a complete metric space is a Baire space in the subspace topology.

> [!theorem] Theorem 50.1
> Let $X$ be a space having finite dimension. If $Y$ is a closed subspace of $X$, then $Y$ has finite dimension, and $\dim Y \le \dim X$.

> [!theorem] Theorem 50.2 (The Sum Theorem for Dimension)
> Let $X = Y \cup Z$, where $Y$ and $Z$ are closed subspaces of $X$. If $\dim Y \le m$ and $\dim Z \le m$, then $\dim X \le m$.

> [!theorem] Theorem 50.5
> Every compact subspace of $\mathbb{R}^n$ has topological dimension at most $n$.

> [!theorem] Theorem 50.6 (The Embedding Theorem)
> Every compact metrizable space of topological dimension $m$ can be embedded in $\mathbb{R}^{2m+1}$.
