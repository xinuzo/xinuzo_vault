>[!tips] 5.1 Derivative
>Let $f$ be defined on $[a, b]$. For $x \in [a, b]$, define the derivative $f'(x)$ as:
>$$f'(x) = \lim_{t \to x} \frac{f(t) - f(x)}{t - x}$$
>provided the limit exists.

>[!tips] 5.7 Local Maximum/Minimum
>$f$ has a local maximum at $p$ if there exists $\delta > 0$ such that $f(q) \le f(p)$ for all $q \in (p-\delta, p+\delta)$.
