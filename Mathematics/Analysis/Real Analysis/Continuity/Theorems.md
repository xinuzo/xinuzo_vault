>[!tips] Theorem 4.2 Limit Sequence Characterization
>$\lim_{x\to p} f(x) = q$ if and only if $\lim_{n\to\infty} f(p_n) = q$ for every sequence $\{p_n\}$ in $E$ such that $p_n \ne p$ and $p_n \to p$.

>[!tips] Theorem 4.6 Continuity and Sequences
>$f$ is continuous at $p$ if and only if $\lim_{n\to\infty} f(p_n) = f(p)$ for every sequence $p_n \to p$.

>[!tips] Theorem 4.8 Open Sets Characterization
>A mapping $f: X \to Y$ is continuous on $X$ if and only if $f^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$ for every open set $V$ in $Y$.

>[!tips] Theorem 4.9 Continuous Image of Compact Sets
>If $f$ is a continuous mapping of a compact metric space $X$ into a metric space $Y$, then $f(X)$ is compact.

>[!tips] Theorem 4.16 Extreme Value Theorem
>If $f$ is a continuous real function on a compact set $X$, then $f$ attains its maximum and minimum values on $X$.

>[!tips] Theorem 4.19 Uniform Continuity on Compact Sets
>If $f$ is a continuous mapping of a compact metric space $X$ into a metric space $Y$, then $f$ is uniformly continuous on $X$.

>[!tips] Theorem 4.22 Continuous Image of Connected Sets
>If $f: X \to Y$ is continuous and $X$ is connected, then $f(X)$ is connected.
>Corollary (Intermediate Value Theorem): If $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ and $f(a) < c < f(b)$, there exists $x \in (a, b)$ such that $f(x) = c$.

>[!tips] Theorem 4.29 Discontinuities of Monotonic Functions
>If $f$ is monotonic on $(a, b)$, then the set of points of discontinuity of $f$ is at most countable.
