>[!tips] Theorem 6.6 Condition for Integrability
>$f \in \mathscr{R}(\alpha)$ on $[a, b]$ if and only if for every $\epsilon > 0$ there exists a partition $P$ such that $U(P, f, \alpha) - L(P, f, \alpha) < \epsilon$.

>[!tips] Theorem 6.8 Continuous Functions are Integrable
>If $f$ is continuous on $[a, b]$, then $f \in \mathscr{R}(\alpha)$.

>[!tips] Theorem 6.9 Monotonic Functions are Integrable
>If $f$ is monotonic on $[a, b]$ and $\alpha$ is continuous, then $f \in \mathscr{R}(\alpha)$.

>[!tips] Theorem 6.12 Properties
>(a) $\int (f_1 + f_2) d\alpha = \int f_1 d\alpha + \int f_2 d\alpha$.
>(b) $\int c f d\alpha = c \int f d\alpha$.
>(c) If $f_1 \le f_2$, then $\int f_1 d\alpha \le \int f_2 d\alpha$.
>(d) $|\int f d\alpha| \le \int |f| d\alpha$.

>[!tips] Theorem 6.20 Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
>If $f \in \mathscr{R}$ on $[a, b]$ and $F(x) = \int_a^x f(t) dt$, then $F$ is continuous. If $f$ is continuous at $x_0$, then $F$ is differentiable at $x_0$ and $F'(x_0) = f(x_0)$.
>If $g' \in \mathscr{R}$ on $[a, b]$, then $\int_a^b g'(x) dx = g(b) - g(a)$.
