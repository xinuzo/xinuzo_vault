>[!tips] 3.1.3 Definition (Convergence)
> A sequence $X= (x_{n})$ in $\mathbb{R}$ is said to **converge** to $x \in \mathbb{R}$, or $x$ is said to be a **limit** of $(X_{n})$, if  $\forall \epsilon  > 0$, $\exists K(\epsilon)$ such that  $\forall n\geq K(\epsilon)$, the terms $x_{n}$ satisfy $|x_{n}-x|<\epsilon.$
