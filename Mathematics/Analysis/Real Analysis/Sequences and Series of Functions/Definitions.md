>[!tips] 7.1 Pointwise Convergence
>$\{f_n\}$ converges to $f$ pointwise on $E$ if for every $x \in E$, $\lim_{n\to\infty} f_n(x) = f(x)$.

>[!tips] 7.7 Uniform Convergence
>$\{f_n\}$ converges uniformly to $f$ on $E$ if for every $\epsilon > 0$ there is an integer $N$ such that $n \ge N$ implies $|f_n(x) - f(x)| < \epsilon$ for all $x \in E$.

>[!tips] 7.19 Equicontinuous Family
>A family $\mathscr{F}$ of functions is equicontinuous on $E$ if for every $\epsilon > 0$ there exists $\delta > 0$ such that $|f(x) - f(y)| < \epsilon$ whenever $d(x, y) < \delta, x \in E, y \in E, f \in \mathscr{F}$.

>[!tips] 7.28 Algebra of Functions
>A set $\mathscr{A}$ of functions is an algebra if it is closed under addition, multiplication, and scalar multiplication.

>[!tips] 8.1 Analytic Functions
>Functions representable by power series.

>[!tips] 8.6 Exponential and Logarithm
>$E(z) = \sum z^n/n!$. $L(z)$ is the inverse of $E(z)$ (on restricted domain).

>[!tips] 8.6 Trigonometric Functions
>$C(x) = (E(ix) + E(-ix))/2$, $S(x) = (E(ix) - E(-ix))/(2i)$.

>[!tips] 8.10 Fourier Series
>For $f$ periodic with period $2\pi$, $f(x) \sim \sum_{-\infty}^\infty c_n e^{inx}$ where $c_n = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\pi}^\pi f(x) e^{-inx} dx$.

>[!tips] 8.17 Gamma Function
>$\Gamma(x) = \int_0^\infty t^{x-1} e^{-t} dt$ for $x > 0$.
