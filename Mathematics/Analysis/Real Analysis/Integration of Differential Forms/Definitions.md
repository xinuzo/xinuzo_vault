>[!tips] 10.2 Support
>The support of a function $f$, $\text{supp}(f)$, is the closure of $\{x : f(x) \ne 0\}$.

>[!tips] 10.10 Differential Form
>A $k$-form in $E \subset \mathbb{R}^n$ is a function $\omega$ represented symbolically as $\sum a_I(x) dx_I$, where $I$ is an increasing $k$-index.

>[!tips] 10.11 Wedge Product
>An operation $\wedge$ that is associative, distributive, and anticommutative ($dx_i \wedge dx_j = -dx_j \wedge dx_i$).

>[!tips] 10.20 Exterior Derivative
>For a $k$-form $\omega = \sum f_I dx_I$, the differential is $d\omega = \sum df_I \wedge dx_I$.

>[!tips] 10.33 Simplex and Chain
>A simplex is a generalization of a triangle/tetrahedron. A chain is a formal sum of simplexes.
