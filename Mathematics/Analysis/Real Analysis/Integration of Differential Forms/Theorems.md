>[!tips] Theorem 10.9 Change of Variables
>If $T$ is a $C^1$ diffeomorphism, $\int_{T(E)} f(y) dy = \int_E f(T(x)) |J_T(x)| dx$.

>[!tips] Theorem 10.23 Properties of $d$
>$d(\omega \wedge \lambda) = (d\omega) \wedge \lambda + (-1)^k \omega \wedge d\lambda$.
>$d^2 = 0$ (i.e., $d(d\omega) = 0$).

>[!tips] Theorem 10.33 Stokes' Theorem
>If $\Psi$ is a $k$-chain and $\omega$ is a $(k-1)$-form:
>$$\int_\Psi d\omega = \int_{\partial \Psi} \omega$$
>(Generalizes Fundamental Theorem of Calculus, Green's Theorem, Divergence Theorem).

>[!tips] Theorem 10.39 Closed and Exact Forms
>$\omega$ is exact if $\omega = d\eta$. $\omega$ is closed if $d\omega = 0$.
>Every exact form is closed ($d^2=0$). Converse (Poincare Lemma) holds in convex sets.
