>[!tips] Theorem 9.7 Norm of Operator
>If $A \in L(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^m)$, $\|A\| = \sup \{|Ax| : |x| \le 1\}$ is finite and makes $L(\mathbb{R}^n, \mathbb{R}^m)$ a metric space.

>[!tips] Theorem 9.15 Chain Rule
>If $F(x) = g(f(x))$, then $F'(x) = g'(f(x))f'(x)$ (matrix multiplication).

>[!tips] Theorem 9.21 Continuously Differentiable ($C^1$)
>$f \in C^1(E)$ if and only if the partial derivatives $D_j f_i$ exist and are continuous on $E$.

>[!tips] Theorem 9.23 Contraction Principle
>If $X$ is a complete metric space and $\phi$ is a contraction, there exists a unique fixed point $x$ such that $\phi(x) = x$.

>[!tips] Theorem 9.24 Inverse Function Theorem
>If $f \in C^1(E)$, $f'(a)$ is invertible, then there exist open sets $U \ni a, V \ni f(a)$ such that $f: U \to V$ is a bijection with a differentiable inverse.

>[!tips] Theorem 9.28 Implicit Function Theorem
>Conditions under which an equation $f(x, y) = 0$ can be solved for $y$ in terms of $x$ (locally).
