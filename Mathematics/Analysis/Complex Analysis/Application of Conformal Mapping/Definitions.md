>[!tips] 10.107 Dirichlet Problem
>The problem of finding a harmonic function in a domain $D$ that takes prescribed values on the boundary of $D$.

>[!tips] 10.107 Steady Temperatures
>Temperature $T(x, y)$ in a thin plate with insulated faces satisfies Laplace's equation $\nabla^2 T = 0$. Isotherms are level curves $T = c$.

>[!tips] 10.112 Electrostatic Potential
>The electric potential $V(x, y)$ in a 2D field satisfies $\nabla^2 V = 0$. Flux lines are orthogonal to equipotential lines.

>[!tips] 10.114 Stream Function
>In 2D fluid flow, the harmonic conjugate $\psi(x, y)$ of the velocity potential $\phi(x, y)$ is the stream function. Curves $\psi = c$ are streamlines (paths of particles).
>Complex Potential: $F(z) = \phi(x, y) + i\psi(x, y)$. Velocity vector $V = \overline{F'(z)}$.
