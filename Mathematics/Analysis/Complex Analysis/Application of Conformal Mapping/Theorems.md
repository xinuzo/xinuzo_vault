>[!tips] 10.108 Transformation of Boundary Conditions
>Conformal mapping simplifies boundary value problems by mapping complicated domains (like a polygon or slit plane) to simpler ones (like the upper half plane or unit disk) where the solution is known.
>- **Dirichlet Condition** ($T = T_0$): Preserved under mapping.
>- **Neumann Condition** ($\partial T / \partial n = 0$): Preserved along boundary curves that are mapped conformally.

>[!tips] 10.116 Bernouilli's Equation
>For steady irrotational incompressible flow, $p/\rho + \frac{1}{2}|V|^2 = \text{constant}$ along a streamline.
