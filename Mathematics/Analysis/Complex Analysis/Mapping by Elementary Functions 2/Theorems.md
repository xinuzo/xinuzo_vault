>[!tips] 8.90 Mapping by $1/z$
>The transformation $w = 1/z$ maps circles and lines to circles and lines. Specifically:
>- Circles through the origin map to lines not through the origin.
>- Lines through the origin map to lines through the origin.
>- Circles not through the origin map to circles not through the origin.

>[!tips] 8.93 Properties of Möbius Transformations
>1.  **Circle Preservation**: Maps the class of circles and lines onto itself.
>2.  **Fixed Points**: Has at most two fixed points unless it is the identity.
>3.  **Symmetry**: Preserves symmetry with respect to circles (inverse points map to inverse points).

>[!tips] 8.95 Mapping of Regions
>Standard elementary functions are used to map specific geometric regions (sectors, strips, etc.) onto simpler domains like the unit disk or upper half plane (essential for solving boundary value problems).
