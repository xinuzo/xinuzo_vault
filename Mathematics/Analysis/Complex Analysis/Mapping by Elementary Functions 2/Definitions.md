>[!tips] 8.90 Linear Transformation
>The mapping $w = Az + B$ ($A \neq 0$) expands or contracts by factor $|A|$, rotates by $\text{arg } A$, and translates by $B$.

>[!tips] 8.93 Linear Fractional Transformation (Möbius)
>A transformation of the form:
>$$w = \frac{az + b}{cz + d}, \quad (ad - bc \neq 0)$$
>Also called bilinear transformation. It can be composed of translation, rotation, magnification, and inversion ($w = 1/z$).

>[!tips] 8.94 Cross Ratio
>For three distinct points $z_1, z_2, z_3$, the mapping that sends them to $w_1, w_2, w_3$ is unique. The cross ratio is preserved:
>$$\frac{(w - w_1)(w_2 - w_3)}{(w - w_3)(w_2 - w_1)} = \frac{(z - z_1)(z_2 - z_3)}{(z - z_3)(z_2 - z_1)}$$

>[!tips] 8.96 Mapping by Exponential/Trigonometric
>- $w = \exp z$ maps infinite strips to sectors or the whole plane.
>- $w = \sin z$ maps semi-infinite strips to quadrants or half-planes.
>- $w = z^2$ maps the first quadrant to the upper half plane.
