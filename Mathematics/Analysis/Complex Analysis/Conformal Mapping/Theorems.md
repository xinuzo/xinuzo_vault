>[!tips] 9.101 Analyticity and Conformality
>If $f$ is analytic at $z_0$ and $f'(z_0) \neq 0$, then the mapping $w = f(z)$ is conformal at $z_0$.

>[!tips] 9.102 Critical Points
>Points where $f'(z) = 0$ are critical points. The mapping is not conformal there; angles are multiplied by $k+1$ where $k$ is the order of the zero of $f'$.

>[!tips] 9.103 Local Inverse
>If $f$ is analytic at $z_0$ and $f'(z_0) \neq 0$, there exists a local inverse transformation $z = g(w)$ defined in a neighborhood of $w_0 = f(z_0)$ which is also analytic.

>[!tips] 9.105 Transformation of Harmonic Functions
>If $h(u, v)$ is harmonic in a domain $D_w$ and $w = f(z) = u(x, y) + iv(x, y)$ is analytic mapping $D_z$ onto $D_w$, then $H(x, y) = h(u(x, y), v(x, y))$ is harmonic in $D_z$.
