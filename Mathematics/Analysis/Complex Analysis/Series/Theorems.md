>[!tips] 5.57 Taylor's Theorem
>If $f$ is analytic throughout a disk $|z - z_0| < R_0$, then $f(z)$ has the power series representation:
>$$f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{f^{(n)}(z_0)}{n!} (z - z_0)^n$$
>valid for $|z - z_0| < R_0$. (Maclaurin series if $z_0 = 0$).

>[!tips] 5.60 Laurent's Theorem
>If $f$ is analytic in an annulus $R_1 < |z - z_0| < R_2$ and $C$ is any closed contour within that annulus, then $f(z)$ has a Laurent series expansion where coefficients are given by integrals over $C$.

>[!tips] 5.65 Uniqueness of Series Representations
>If a series expansion $\sum a_n (z-z_0)^n$ equals $f(z)$, it must be the Taylor series. Similarly for Laurent series.
