>[!tips] 7.78 Improper Integral
>$\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(x) dx = \lim_{R_1 \to \infty} \int_{-R_1}^0 f(x) dx + \lim_{R_2 \to \infty} \int_{0}^{R_2} f(x) dx$.
>Cauchy Principal Value (P.V.): $\lim_{R \to \infty} \int_{-R}^R f(x) dx$.

>[!tips] 7.85 Indented Path
>A contour modified to avoid a singularity on the real axis (usually a small semi-circle of radius $\epsilon$).

>[!tips] 7.86 Argument Principle
>Let $f$ be meromorphic inside and on $C$. The change in argument of $f(z)$ as $z$ traverses $C$ is $\Delta_C \arg f(z) = 2\pi(Z - P)$, where $Z$ is zeros and $P$ is poles (counting multiplicity).
