>[!tips] 11.126 Polygon
>A polygon in the $w$-plane is a closed chain of line segments. The vertices are $w_1, ..., w_n$.
>A degenerate polygon may include vertices at infinity.

>[!tips] 11.127 Exterior Angle
>At each vertex $w_k$, the change in angle of the tangent is the exterior angle $k_k \pi$, where $-1 < k_k < 1$.
>The sum of exterior angles is $2\pi$ (for a closed polygon).

>[!tips] 11.127 Schwarz-Christoffel Transformation
>A transformation mapping the real axis of the $z$-plane onto the boundary of a polygon in the $w$-plane. The interior of the polygon maps to the upper half of the $z$-plane.
