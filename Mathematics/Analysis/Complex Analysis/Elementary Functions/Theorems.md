>[!tips] 3.29 Properties of $\exp(z)$
>$\exp(z_1 + z_2) = \exp(z_1)\exp(z_2)$.
>$\exp(z) \neq 0$ for any $z$.
>$|\exp(z)| = e^x$, $\arg(\exp(z)) = y + 2n\pi$.

>[!tips] 3.31 Analyticity of Log
>$Log z$ is analytic in the domain $D^* = \mathbb{C} \setminus (-\infty, 0]$.
>$\frac{d}{dz} Log z = \frac{1}{z}$.

>[!tips] 3.33 Properties of Trig Functions
>$\sin(z_1+z_2) = \sin z_1 \cos z_2 + \cos z_1 \sin z_2$.
>Zeros of $\sin z$ are real ($n\pi$). Zeros of $\cos z$ are real ($\pi/2 + n\pi$).
>Analytic everywhere (entire).
