>[!tips] Theorem 1.1 Degree Sum Formula
>For any graph $G$, $\sum_{v \in V} d(v) = 2m$.

>[!tips] Corollary 1.2 Handshaking Lemma
>In any graph, the number of vertices of odd degree is even.

>[!tips] Proposition 1.3 Bipartite Degrees
>Let $G[X, Y]$ be a bipartite graph without isolated vertices such that $d(x) \ge d(y)$ for all $xy \in E$ ($x \in X, y \in Y$). Then $|X| \le |Y|$, with equality iff $d(x) = d(y)$ for all edges.

>[!tips] Principle of Directional Duality
>Any statement about a digraph has a dual statement obtained by applying it to the converse digraph (reversing all arcs).
>Example: $\sum d^-(v) = m \implies \sum d^+(v) = m$.

>[!tips] Theorem 1.2.15 (Polya)
>The number of distinct labellings of a given unlabelled simple graph $G$ on $n$ vertices is $n!/aut(G)$.

>[!tips] Sperner's Lemma (1.3.12)
>In a simplicial subdivision of a triangle $T$ with coloured vertices (red, blue, green on sides properly), the number of triangles with all three colours is odd.
