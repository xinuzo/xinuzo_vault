>[!tips] Theorem 11.2 The Five-Colour Theorem
>Every plane graph is 5-face-colourable. (Heawood, 1890).
>Proof uses Euler's formula to find a vertex of degree $\le 5$ and Kempe chains to resolve the case of degree 5.

>[!tips] Theorem 11.1 The Four-Colour Theorem
>Every plane graph is 4-face-colourable. (Appel and Haken, 1977).
>Proof involves finding an unavoidable set of reducible configurations and checking them by computer.

>[!tips] Theorem 11.4 Tait's Theorem
>A 3-connected cubic plane graph has a 4-face-colouring if and only if it is 3-edge-colourable.
