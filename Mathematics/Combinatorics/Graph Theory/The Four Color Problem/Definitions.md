>[!tips] 11.1 k-Face Colouring
>An assignment of $k$ colours to the faces of a plane graph such that no two adjacent faces receive the same colour. Equivalent to vertex colouring the dual graph.

>[!tips] 11.1 Kempe Chain
>In a coloured graph, a component of the subgraph induced by vertices of two specific colours (say Red and Green). Swapping colours on a Kempe chain produces a new valid colouring.
