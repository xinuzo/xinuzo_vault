>[!tips] Theorem 2.1 Max Cut Bound
>Every loopless graph $G$ has a bipartite subgraph with at least $m/2$ edges.

>[!tips] Theorem 2.3 Rédei's Theorem
>Every tournament has a directed Hamilton path.

>[!tips] Theorem 2.8 Graham-Pollak
>If $K_n$ is decomposed into $k$ complete bipartite graphs, then $k \ge n-1$.

>[!tips] Theorem 2.17 Even Subgraphs
>A set of edges is an even subgraph if and only if it is a disjoint union of cycles.

>[!tips] Proposition 2.18 Orthogonality
>Every even subgraph meets every edge cut in an even number of edges. (Cycle space and Bond space are orthogonal complements).

>[!tips] Lemma 2.20 Kelly's Lemma
>For any two graphs $F$ and $G$ with $v(F) < v(G)$, the number of copies of $F$ in $G$ is a reconstructible parameter.
>Formula: $\binom{G}{F} = \frac{1}{v(G)-v(F)} \sum_{v \in V} \binom{G-v}{F}$.

>[!tips] Conjecture 2.19 Reconstruction Conjecture
>Every simple graph on at least three vertices is reconstructible.
