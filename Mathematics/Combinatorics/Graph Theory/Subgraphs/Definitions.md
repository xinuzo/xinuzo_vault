>[!tips] 2.1 Subgraph
>$F$ is a subgraph of $G$ ($F \subseteq G$) if $V(F) \subseteq V(G)$ and $E(F) \subseteq E(G)$ (and incidence is preserved). $G$ is a supergraph of $F$.

>[!tips] 2.1 Spanning Subgraph
>A subgraph obtained by edge deletions only (same vertex set as $G$). Denoted $G \setminus S$ where $S$ is deleted edges.

>[!tips] 2.1 Induced Subgraph
>For $X \subseteq V$, the subgraph induced by $X$, denoted $G[X]$, has vertex set $X$ and all edges of $G$ with both ends in $X$.

>[!tips] 2.1 Girth and Circumference
>- **Girth ($g(G)$)**: Length of a shortest cycle.
>- **Circumference ($c(G)$)**: Length of a longest cycle.

>[!tips] 2.2 Join
>The join $G \vee H$ is obtained from the disjoint union $G + H$ by adding edges joining every vertex of $G$ to every vertex of $H$.

>[!tips] 2.4 Decomposition and Covering
>- **Decomposition**: A family of edge-disjoint subgraphs whose union is $G$.
>- **Covering**: A family of subgraphs whose union is $G$ (edges may overlap).

>[!tips] 2.6 Even Subgraph / Edge Cut
>- **Even Subgraph**: A subgraph where every vertex has even degree.
>- **Edge Cut ($\partial(X)$)**: Set of edges with one end in $X$ and one in $V \setminus X$.

>[!tips] 2.6 Cycle and Bond Spaces
>- **Edge Space $\mathcal{E}(G)$**: Vector space of all subsets of $E$ over $GF(2)$.
>- **Cycle Space $\mathcal{C}(G)$**: Subspace generated by cycles (all even subgraphs).
>- **Bond Space $\mathcal{B}(G)$**: Subspace generated by bonds (all edge cuts).

>[!tips] 2.7 Reconstruction
>- **Hypomorphic**: Graphs $G$ and $H$ are hypomorphic if $G-v \cong H-v$ for all $v$.
>- **Reconstructible**: $G$ is reconstructible if every reconstruction (graph hypomorphic to $G$) is isomorphic to $G$.
