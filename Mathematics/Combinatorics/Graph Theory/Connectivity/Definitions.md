>[!tips] 9.1 Local Connectivity
>The local connectivity $p(x, y)$ is the maximum number of pairwise internally disjoint $xy$-paths.

>[!tips] 9.1 k-connected
>A graph $G$ is $k$-connected if $p(u, v) \ge k$ for all distinct $u, v$. The connectivity $\kappa(G)$ is the maximum $k$.

>[!tips] 9.1 Vertex Cut
>An $xy$-vertex-cut is a subset $S \subseteq V \setminus \{x, y\}$ separating $x$ and $y$. Local cut function $c(x, y)$ is the min size of such a cut.

>[!tips] 9.3 Edge Connectivity
>$\kappa'(G)$: The minimum number of edges whose deletion disconnects $G$.
>Local edge connectivity $p'(x, y)$ is the max number of edge-disjoint paths.
