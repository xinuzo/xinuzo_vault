>[!tips] Theorem 9.1 Menger's Theorem (Vertex)
>In any graph $G(x, y)$ where $x, y$ are nonadjacent, the maximum number of pairwise internally disjoint $xy$-paths is equal to the minimum number of vertices in an $xy$-vertex-cut ($p(x, y) = c(x, y)$).

>[!tips] Theorem 9.2 Global Connectivity
>If $G$ has nonadjacent vertices, $\kappa(G) = \min \{ p(u, v) : u, v \in V, u \neq v, uv \notin E \}$.

>[!tips] Lemma 9.3 Connectivity Extension
>If $G$ is $k$-connected and $H$ is obtained by adding a vertex joined to at least $k$ vertices of $G$, then $H$ is $k$-connected.

>[!tips] Proposition 9.5 The Fan Lemma
>Let $G$ be $k$-connected, $x$ a vertex, and $Y$ a set of $\ge k$ vertices. Then there exists a $k$-fan from $x$ to $Y$ ( $k$ paths from $x$ to distinct vertices in $Y$, disjoint except at $x$).

>[!tips] Theorem 9.6 Dirac's Cycle Theorem
>In a $k$-connected graph ($k \ge 2$), any set of $k$ vertices lies on a common cycle.

>[!tips] Theorem 9.7 Menger's Theorem (Edge Version)
>$p'(x, y) = c'(x, y)$ (Max edge-disjoint paths = Min edge cut).
