>[!tips] Lemma 13.1 The Crossing Lemma
>If $G$ is a simple graph with $m \ge 4n$, then the crossing number satisfies:
>$$cr(G) \ge \frac{1}{64} \frac{m^3}{n^2}$$

>[!tips] Theorem 13.5 Markov's Inequality
>If $X$ is a non-negative random variable and $t > 0$, then $P(X \ge t) \le E[X]/t$.

>[!tips] Theorem 13.14 Lovász Local Lemma (Symmetric)
>Let $A_1, ..., A_n$ be events with dependency graph of max degree $d$. If $P(A_i) \le 1/(e(d+1))$, then $P(\cap \bar{A_i}) > 0$ (i.e., there is a nonzero probability that none of the events occur).

>[!tips] Application: Linear Arboricity (Alon)
>The linear arboricity of a $2r$-regular graph of large girth is exactly $r+1$.
