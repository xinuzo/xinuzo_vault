>[!tips] 13.1 Random Graph $G_{n,p}$
>A probability space of graphs on $n$ vertices where each edge is chosen independently with probability $p$.

>[!tips] 13.2 Expectation
>The sum of the probabilities of outcomes weighted by their values. **Linearity of Expectation**: $E[\sum X_i] = \sum E[X_i]$.

>[!tips] 13.4 Threshold Function
>A function $r(n)$ is a threshold function for a property $P$ if, for $p(n) \ll r(n)$, $G_{n,p}$ almost surely does not have $P$, and for $p(n) \gg r(n)$, it almost surely does.
