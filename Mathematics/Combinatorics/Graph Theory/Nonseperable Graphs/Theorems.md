>[!tips] Theorem 5.1 Internally Disjoint Paths
>A connected graph on three or more vertices has no cut vertices if and only if any two distinct vertices are connected by two internally disjoint paths.

>[!tips] Theorem 5.2 Characterization of Nonseparable Graphs
>A connected graph is nonseparable if and only if any two of its edges lie on a common cycle.

>[!tips] Proposition 5.3 Block Decomposition
>The blocks of $G$ form a decomposition of $G$. Any two blocks have at most one vertex in common.

>[!tips] Theorem 5.15 Strong Spanning Subdigraphs
>Every strong digraph $D$ has a strong spanning subdigraph with at most $2n - 2$ arcs.
