>[!tips] 17.1 Chromatic Index $\chi'(G)$
>The minimum number of colours needed to colour the edges of $G$ such that no two incident edges receive the same colour.

>[!tips] 17.2 Class 1 and Class 2
>Graphs with $\chi'(G) = \Delta(G)$ are **Class 1**.
>Graphs with $\chi'(G) = \Delta(G) + 1$ are **Class 2**.

>[!tips] 17.3 Snark
>A cubic graph that is Class 2 (requires 4 colours) is often called a snark (specifically, cyclically 4-edge-connected cubic Class 2 graphs). Example: The Petersen Graph.
