>[!tips] 6.1 Tree-Search
>A procedure generating a sequence of rooted trees, starting with a root, and adding edges/vertices to grow the tree.

>[!tips] 6.1 Breadth-First Search (BFS)
>A tree-search where adjacency lists are scanned on a first-come first-served basis (using a Queue). Produces a level function $\ell(v)$ (distance from root).

>[!tips] 6.1 Depth-First Search (DFS)
>A tree-search where the most recently added vertex is processed first (using a Stack). Produces time functions $f(v)$ (found) and $l(v)$ (left/backtrack).

>[!tips] 6.2 Minimum-Weight Spanning Tree
>A spanning tree $T$ such that $\sum_{e \in E(T)} w(e)$ is minimized.
