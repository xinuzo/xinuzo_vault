>[!tips] Theorem 10.1 Jordan Curve Theorem
>Any simple closed curve $C$ in the plane partitions the rest of the plane into two disjoint open sets, the interior and the exterior, with $C$ as their common boundary.

>[!tips] Theorem 10.2 Euler's Formula
>For a connected plane graph with $n$ vertices, $m$ edges, and $f$ faces:
>$$n - m + f = 2$$

>[!tips] Corollary 10.3 Planarity Bounds
>If $G$ is a simple planar graph with $n \ge 3$, then $m \le 3n - 6$.
>If $G$ is triangle-free and planar ($n \ge 3$), then $m \le 2n - 4$.
>(This implies $K_5$ and $K_{3,3}$ are nonplanar).

>[!tips] Theorem 10.28 Whitney's Theorem
>Every simple 3-connected planar graph has a unique planar embedding (up to homeomorphism of the sphere).

>[!tips] Theorem 10.30 Kuratowski's Theorem
>A graph is planar if and only if it contains no minor isomorphic to $K_5$ or $K_{3,3}$.
>(Equivalent to Wagner's Theorem).

>[!tips] Theorem 10.35 Planarity Testing
>There exists a linear-time algorithm to test if a graph is planar (Hopcroft and Tarjan, 1974).
