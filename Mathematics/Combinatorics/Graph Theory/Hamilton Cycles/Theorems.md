>[!tips] Theorem 18.1 Dirac's Theorem
>If $G$ is a simple graph with $n \ge 3$ and $\delta(G) \ge n/2$, then $G$ is Hamiltonian.

>[!tips] Theorem 18.3 Bondy-Chvátal Theorem
>$G$ is Hamiltonian if and only if its closure $cl(G)$ is Hamiltonian.

>[!tips] Theorem 18.9 Grinberg's Theorem
>A necessary condition for a plane graph to be Hamiltonian, based on the lengths of its faces: $\sum_{f_i} (i-2)(f_i - f'_i) = 0$, where $f_i$ is the number of $i$-gonal faces inside the cycle and $f'_i$ outside.
