>[!tips] Theorem 19.1 Gallai-Milgram Theorem
>For any digraph $D$, $\pi(D) \le \alpha(D)$. (The vertices can be covered by at most $\alpha$ disjoint paths).

>[!tips] Theorem 19.2 Dilworth's Theorem
>In any poset, the minimum size of a path partition (chain decomposition) equals the maximum size of an antichain.

>[!tips] Theorem 19.6 Lucchesi-Younger Theorem
>In any digraph, the maximum number of disjoint directed cuts is equal to the minimum size of a dijoin.
