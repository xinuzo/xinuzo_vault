>[!tips] 19.1 Path Partition
>A partition of the vertices of a digraph into directed paths. $\pi(D)$ is the minimum number of paths.

>[!tips] 19.1 Stability Number $\alpha(D)$
>In a digraph, the size of the largest stable set.

>[!tips] 19.3 Dijoin
>A subset of arcs that intersects every directed cut.
