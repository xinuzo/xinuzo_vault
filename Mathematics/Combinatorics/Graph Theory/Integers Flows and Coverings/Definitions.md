>[!tips] 21.1 Integer Flow
>A nowhere-zero $k$-flow on $G$ is an assignment of values from $\{ \pm 1, \dots, \pm(k-1) \}$ to the edges (given an orientation) such that the net flow at every vertex is zero.

>[!tips] 21.2 Flow Number $\phi(G)$
>The smallest $k$ for which $G$ admits a nowhere-zero $k$-flow.

>[!tips] 21.4 Cycle Cover
>A family of cycles that covers every edge of $G$.
