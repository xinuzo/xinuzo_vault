>[!tips] Theorem 20.1 Matrix Tree Theorem
>The number of spanning trees of $G$ is equal to any cofactor of its Laplacian matrix $L$.

>[!tips] Theorem 20.4 Squaring the Square
>Relates electrical networks to the dissection of rectangles into squares (Smith diagrams).

>[!tips] Connection to Random Walks
>The effective resistance between $u$ and $v$ is proportional to the commute time (expected time for a random walk to go $u \to v \to u$).
