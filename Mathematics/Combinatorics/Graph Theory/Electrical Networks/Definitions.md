>[!tips] 20.1 Laplacian Matrix
>$L = D - A$, where $D$ is the diagonal matrix of degrees and $A$ is the adjacency matrix.

>[!tips] 20.2 Current and Potential
>Functions on edges (current) and vertices (potential) satisfying Kirchhoff's Laws (Current Law: flow conservation; Voltage Law: potential differences sum to 0 around cycles).
