>[!tips] Theorem 3.1 The Friendship Theorem
>Let $G$ be a simple graph in which any two vertices (people) have exactly one common neighbour (friend). Then $G$ has a vertex of degree $n-1$ (a politician, everyone's friend).

>[!tips] Conjecture 3.11 Small Cycle Double Cover Conjecture
>Every simple graph without cut edges has a double cover by at most $n-1$ cycles.

>[!tips] Conjecture 3.12 Oriented Cycle Double Cover Conjecture
>Let $G$ be a graph without cut edges. Then the associated digraph $D(G)$ admits a decomposition into directed cycles of length at least three.
