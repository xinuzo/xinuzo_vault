>[!question] PR 1 25/02/2026
>Show that Dominant Strategy Equilibrium $\implies$ Nash Equilibrium, but the converse is not ture. 

>[!note] Dominant Strategy Equilibrium
A strategy profile consisting of each player’s dominant strategy. The strategy $s^*$ is a **dominant strategy** if it is a player’s **strictly best response** to any strategies the other players might pick, in the sense that whatever strategies they pick, his payoff is highest with $s^*$. Mathematically, \
$$ \pi_{i}(s^*_{i},s^*_{-i}) > \pi_{i}(s'_{i},s^*_{-i}) \quad \forall s'_{i}$$


**dominant-strategy equilibrium** is a strategy profile consisting of each player’s **dominant strategy**.

>[!note] Nash Equilibrium
>No player has incentive to deviate from his strategy given that the other players do not deviate. Formally, \
>$$\forall i, \quad \pi_{i}(s^*_{i},s^*_{-i})\geq \pi_{i}(s'_{i},s^*_{-i}) \quad \forall s'_{i}$$
