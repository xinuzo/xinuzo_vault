>[!tips] Theorem 10.2.1 Asymptotic Distribution of Signed-Rank
>Under $H_0$ (symmetric pdf about 0), the standardized signed-rank statistic converges to $N(0, 1)$ as $n \to \infty$.

>[!tips] Theorem 10.8.1 Adaptive Estimation
>Methods that first select a model (e.g., examining tail weight) and then choose an appropriate robust estimator (like trimmed mean or median) can achieve asymptotic efficiency close to the best estimator for the true distribution.
