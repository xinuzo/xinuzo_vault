>[!tips] 7.1 Second Fundamental Form ($II$)
>A quadratic form on $T_P S$ measuring how the surface pulls away from its tangent plane.
>$$II(\mathbf{v}) = L\lambda^2 + 2M\lambda\mu + N\mu^2$$
>Coefficients: $L = \sigma_{uu} \cdot \mathbf{N}$, $M = \sigma_{uv} \cdot \mathbf{N}$, $N = \sigma_{vv} \cdot \mathbf{N}$.

>[!tips] 7.2 Gauss Map
>The map $\mathcal{G}: S \to S^2$ (unit sphere) defined by $\mathcal{G}(P) = \mathbf{N}(P)$. It measures how the normal vector turns.
>

>[!tips] 7.2 Weingarten Map ($W$)
>The linear map $W = -d\mathcal{G}: T_P S \to T_P S$. Its eigenvalues are the principal curvatures.
>Matrix form: $\begin{pmatrix} L & M \\ M & N \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} E & F \\ F & G \end{pmatrix}^{-1}$.

>[!tips] 7.3 Normal Curvature ($\kappa_n$)
>The curvature of the curve formed by intersecting the surface with the plane spanned by the normal $\mathbf{N}$ and a tangent vector $\mathbf{v}$.
>$$\kappa_n = \frac{II(\mathbf{v})}{I(\mathbf{v})}$$

>[!tips] 7.3 Geodesic Curvature ($\kappa_g$)
>The component of the curvature of a curve on $S$ that is tangent to the surface. It measures how much the curve deviates from being a geodesic.
