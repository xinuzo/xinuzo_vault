>[!tips] Proposition 6.1 Arc Length on Surface
>The length of a curve $\gamma(t) = \sigma(u(t), v(t))$ on a surface is:
>$$L = \int_a^b \sqrt{E\dot{u}^2 + 2F\dot{u}\dot{v} + G\dot{v}^2} dt$$

>[!tips] Theorem 6.4 Archimedes' Theorem
>The map projecting the sphere onto the circumscribing cylinder (projecting horizontally away from the axis) is an **equiareal map** (preserves area).
