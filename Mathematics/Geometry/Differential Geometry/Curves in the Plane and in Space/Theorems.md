>[!tips] Proposition 1.1 Tangent to Level Curves
>The tangent vector to the level curve $f(x, y) = c$ at a point $(x, y)$ is orthogonal to the gradient vector $\nabla f(x, y)$.

>[!tips] Proposition 1.3 Regularity Preservation
>If $\gamma$ is a regular curve and $\tilde{\gamma}$ is a reparametrization of $\gamma$, then $\tilde{\gamma}$ is also regular.
