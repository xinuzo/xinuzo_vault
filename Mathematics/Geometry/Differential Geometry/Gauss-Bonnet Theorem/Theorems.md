>[!tips] Theorem 10.1 Local Gauss-Bonnet Theorem
>For a geodesic triangle $T$ on a surface:
>$$\iint_T K dA = (\alpha + \beta + \gamma) - \pi$$
>The integral of the Gaussian curvature over the triangle is equal to the "angle excess".
>(For a sphere ($K>0$), sum of angles $> \pi$. For a pseudosphere ($K<0$), sum of angles $< \pi$).

>[!tips] Theorem 10.4 Global Gauss-Bonnet Theorem
>For a compact, orientable surface $S$ (without boundary):
>$$\iint_S K dA = 2\pi \chi(S)$$
>where $\chi(S)$ is the Euler Characteristic ($\chi = 2 - 2g$, $g$ is genus).
>This links geometry (curvature) to topology (Euler characteristic).
