>[!tips] Theorem 8.1 Theorema Egregium (Gauss's Remarkable Theorem)
>Gaussian curvature $K$ depends only on the First Fundamental Form ($E, F, G$ and their derivatives).
>This means $K$ is an **intrinsic invariant**; it remains unchanged under local isometry (bending without stretching).
>Formula (if $F=0$): $K = -\frac{1}{2\sqrt{EG}} \left( \frac{\partial}{\partial u} \left( \frac{G_u}{\sqrt{EG}} \right) + \frac{\partial}{\partial v} \left( \frac{E_v}{\sqrt{EG}} \right) \right)$.

>[!tips] Theorem 8.4 Minding's Theorem
>Two surfaces with the same constant Gaussian curvature $K$ are locally isometric.
>(This explains why a sphere ($K > 0$) cannot be mapped to a plane ($K=0$) without distortion).
