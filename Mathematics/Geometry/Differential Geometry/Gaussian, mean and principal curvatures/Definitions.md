>[!tips] 8.1 Gaussian Curvature ($K$)
>The determinant of the Weingarten map $W$.
>$$K = \det(W) = \frac{LN - M^2}{EG - F^2}$$
>It is the product of the principal curvatures: $K = \kappa_1 \kappa_2$.

>[!tips] 8.1 Mean Curvature ($H$)
>The trace of the Weingarten map (divided by 2).
>$$H = \frac{1}{2}\text{trace}(W) = \frac{1}{2} \frac{LG - 2MF + NE}{EG - F^2}$$
>It is the average of the principal curvatures: $H = \frac{\kappa_1 + \kappa_2}{2}$.

>[!tips] 8.3 Flat / Minimal Surface
>- **Flat Surface**: $K = 0$ everywhere (e.g., plane, cylinder, cone).
>- **Minimal Surface**: $H = 0$ everywhere (e.g., helicoid, catenoid, Enneper's surface).

>[!tips] 8.5 Principal Curvatures
>The eigenvalues $\kappa_1, \kappa_2$ of the Weingarten map.
>They are the maximum and minimum values of the normal curvature $\kappa_n$ at a point.
