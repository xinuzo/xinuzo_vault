>[!tips] 5.1 Level Surface
>A surface defined implicitly by an equation $f(x, y, z) = c$.
>It is regular at points where the gradient $\nabla f \neq \mathbf{0}$.

>[!tips] 5.2 Quadric Surface
>A level surface of a quadratic polynomial in $x, y, z$.
>Examples: Ellipsoid, Hyperboloid of one sheet, Hyperboloid of two sheets, Elliptic Paraboloid, Hyperbolic Paraboloid (Saddle).

>[!tips] 5.3 Surface of Revolution
>Generated by rotating a curve (profile curve) in the $xz$-plane about the $z$-axis.
>Parametrization: $\sigma(u, v) = (f(u)\cos v, f(u)\sin v, g(u))$.

>[!tips] 5.3 Ruled Surface
>A surface formed by a family of straight lines (rulings).
>Parametrization: $\sigma(u, v) = \gamma(u) + v\delta(u)$, where $\gamma$ is a base curve and $\delta$ is the direction of the line.
>Examples: Cylinder, Cone, Hyperboloid of one sheet, Helicoid.
>

>[!tips] 5.4 Compact Surface
>A surface that is closed and bounded in $\mathbb{R}^3$ (e.g., Sphere, Torus).
