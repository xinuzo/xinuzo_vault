>[!tips] Proposition 4.3 Transition Maps
>If $\sigma$ and $\tilde{\sigma}$ are two regular surface patches for the same part of a surface, the map $\Phi = \tilde{\sigma}^{-1} \circ \sigma$ (the transition map) is a smooth diffeomorphism (a smooth map with a smooth inverse).

>[!tips] Proposition 4.5 Characterization of Orientability
>A connected surface is orientable if and only if it admits an atlas where the Jacobian determinant of every transition map is positive.
