>[!tips] 15.1 Map Colouring
>A colouring of the faces of a map on a surface $S$ such that adjacent faces (sharing an edge) receive different colours.

>[!tips] 15.1 Euler Characteristic
>For a surface $S$, the Euler characteristic is $\chi(S) = v - e + f$.
>For a sphere, $\chi=2$; for a torus, $\chi=0$.

>[!tips] 15.2 Hadwiger's Conjecture
>Every $k$-chromatic graph has a $K_k$-minor. (Generalization of the Four-Colour Theorem, where $k=5$ implies planar graphs have a $K_5$ minor).

>[!tips] 15.5 List Colouring of Surfaces
>Extensions of list colouring to graphs embedded on surfaces.
