# Theorems - Chapter XVII: Explicit Formulas

## §3. The Basic Sum

>[!tips] 17.3 Explicit Formula (Weil)
>A relation connecting a sum over the zeros $\rho$ of the Zeta (or L-) function and a sum over the prime powers of the field:
>$$\sum_{\rho} \Phi(\rho) = \dots - \sum_{\mathfrak{p}, m} \frac{\log N\mathfrak{p}}{N\mathfrak{p}^{m/2}} F(\log N\mathfrak{p}^m) + \dots$$
>where $\Phi$ is the Mellin transform of a test function $F$.
