# Theorems - Chapter XVI: The Brauer-Siegel Theorem

## §4. End of the Proofs

>[!tips] 16.4 Brauer-Siegel Theorem
>Let $k$ range over a sequence of number fields normal over $\mathbb{Q}$ such that $[k:\mathbb{Q}]/\log|D_k| \to 0$. Then:
>$$\log(h_k R_k) \sim \log \sqrt{|D_k|}$$
>where $h_k$ is the class number and $R_k$ is the regulator.
