# Theorems - Chapter XII: L-series Again

## §1. Proper Abelian L-series

>[!tips] 12.1 Theorem 1
>The Artin L-series for an abelian extension coincides with the Hecke L-series for the corresponding character of the idele class group.

## §2. Artin L-series

>[!tips] 12.2 Theorem 2
>Assume the Artin conjecture. The L-series $L(s, \chi)$ extends to an entire function (for $\chi \neq 1$) and satisfies a functional equation.
