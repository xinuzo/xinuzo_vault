# Definitions - Chapter XII: L-series Again

## §2. Artin L-series

>[!tips] 12.2 Artin L-series ($L(s, \chi, L/K)$)
>For a Galois extension $L/K$ and character $\chi$ of the Galois group:
>$$L(s, \chi, L/K) = \prod_{\mathfrak{p}} \frac{1}{\det(I - N\mathfrak{p}^{-s} \sigma_{\mathfrak{p}})}$$
>where $\sigma_{\mathfrak{p}}$ is the Frobenius class.
