# Definitions - Chapter IX: Norm Index Computations

## §1. Algebraic Preliminaries

>[!tips] 9.1 Herbrand Quotient
>For a cyclic group $G$ acting on a module $A$, the **Herbrand quotient** is $Q(A) = [H^2(G, A)] / [H^1(G, A)]$.
