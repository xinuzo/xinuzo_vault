# Theorems - Chapter IX: Norm Index Computations

## §3. The Local Norm Index

>[!tips] 9.3 Theorem 1
>Let $L/K$ be cyclic of degree $n$. Then:
>$$[K^* : N_{L/K} L^*] = n$$

## §5. The Global Cyclic Norm Index

>[!tips] 9.5 Theorem 4 (First Inequality)
>Let $L/K$ be cyclic of degree $n$. Then the index of norms in the idele class group satisfies:
>$$[C_K : N_{L/K} C_L] = n$$
>Or equivalently in terms of ideal classes for unramified extensions.
