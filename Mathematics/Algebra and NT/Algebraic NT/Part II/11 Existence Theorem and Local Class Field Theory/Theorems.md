# Theorems - Chapter XI: The Existence Theorem

## §2. Proof of the Existence Theorem

>[!tips] 11.2 Existence Theorem
>For every congruence subgroup $H$ of the idele class group $C_K$ (or ideal group), there exists a unique abelian extension $L/K$ such that $N_{L/K} C_L = H$. The Galois group $Gal(L/K) \cong C_K/H$.

## §5. The Principal Ideal Theorem

>[!tips] 11.5 Principal Ideal Theorem
>Every ideal in $K$ becomes principal in the Hilbert Class Field of $K$.
