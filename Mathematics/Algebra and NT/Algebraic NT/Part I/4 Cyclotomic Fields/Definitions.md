# Definitions - Chapter IV: Cyclotomic Fields

## §1. Roots of Unity

>[!tips] 4.1 Primitive Root
>A root of unity $\omega$ is **primitive** of order $n$ if $\omega^n = 1$ and $\omega^d \neq 1$ for any $d < n$.

>[!tips] 4.1 Cyclotomic Extension
>A **cyclotomic extension** of $K$ is one contained in $K(\omega)$ where $\omega$ is a root of unity. It is an abelian extension.

## §3. Gauss Sums

>[!tips] 4.3 Gauss Sum
>For a primitive character $\chi$ mod $q$ and an integer $n$, the **Gauss sum** is:
>$$\tau(\chi, n) = \sum_{x \text{ mod } q} \chi(x) \zeta^{nx}$$
>where $\zeta$ is a primitive $q$-th root of unity.
