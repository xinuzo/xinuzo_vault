# Theorems - Chapter VIII: Elementary Properties of the Zeta Function

## §2. Zeta Function of a Number Field

>[!tips] 8.2 Theorem 1
>The series for $\zeta_k(s)$ converges absolutely for $Re(s) > 1$ and defines an analytic function. It has a simple pole at $s=1$ with residue $\kappa$ (the class number formula constant).

## §3. The L-Series

>[!tips] 8.3 Theorem 2
>If $\chi$ is non-trivial, $L(s, \chi)$ is regular at $s=1$. If $\chi$ is trivial, it has a simple pole.
