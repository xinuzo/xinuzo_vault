# Definitions - Chapter VI: The Ideal Function

## §1. Generalized Ideal Classes

>[!tips] 6.1 Cycle ($\mathfrak{c}$)
>A **cycle** $\mathfrak{c}$ is a formal product $\prod \mathfrak{p}^{n_{\mathfrak{p}}}$ over all primes (finite and infinite).

>[!tips] 6.1 Congruence Modulo $\mathfrak{c}$
>We write $\alpha \equiv 1 \pmod{\mathfrak{c}}$ if:
>1. $\alpha \equiv 1 \pmod{\mathfrak{p}^{n_{\mathfrak{p}}}}$ for finite primes with $n_{\mathfrak{p}} > 0$.
>2. $\alpha > 0$ at real infinite primes appearing in $\mathfrak{c}$.

>[!tips] 6.1 Ray Class Group
>The group of fractional ideals prime to $\mathfrak{c}$ modulo the subgroup of principal ideals generated by elements $\alpha \equiv 1 \pmod{\mathfrak{c}}$.

## §3. The Number of Ideals

>[!tips] 6.3 Regulator ($R_k$)
>The **regulator** $R_k$ is the absolute value of the determinant of the matrix formed by the logarithms of the fundamental units of the field.
