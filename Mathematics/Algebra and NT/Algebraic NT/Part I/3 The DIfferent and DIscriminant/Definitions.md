# Definitions - Chapter III: The Different and Discriminant

## §1. Complementary Modules

>[!tips] 3.1 Complementary Set ($L'$)
>Let $L$ be an additive subgroup of $E$. The **complementary set** $L'$ (relative to the trace) is the set of $x \in E$ such that $Tr_{K}^{E}(xL) \subset A$.

>[!tips] 3.1 Different ($\mathfrak{D}_{B/A}$)
>The **different** of $B$ over $A$ is defined as $\mathfrak{D}_{B/A} = (B')^{-1}$, where $B'$ is the complementary module of $B$. It is an ideal of $B$.

## §3. The Discriminant

>[!tips] 3.3 Discriminant ($D_{E/K}$)
>Let $W = (w_1, \dots, w_n)$ be a set of elements in $E$. The **discriminant** is:
>$$D_{E/K}(W) = \det(\sigma_i w_j)^2$$
>where $\sigma_i$ ranges over the distinct embeddings of $E$.
>The discriminant of the ring of integers $\mathfrak{o}_K$ is denoted $D_K$.
