# Theorems - Chapter VII: Ideles and Adeles

## §2. Adeles

>[!tips] 7.2 Theorem (Strong Approximation)
>The field $k$ is discrete in $\mathbb{A}_k$, and the quotient $\mathbb{A}_k / k$ is compact.

## §3. Ideles

>[!tips] 7.3 Product Formula (Idelic)
>For any idele $a = (a_v) \in J_k$, the volume of the fundamental domain scales by $|a| = \prod_v |a_v|_v$. The product formula states that for $\alpha \in k^*$, $|\alpha| = 1$.

>[!tips] 7.3 Theorem 2
>The group of idele classes $C_k^1$ (elements of norm 1) is compact.
