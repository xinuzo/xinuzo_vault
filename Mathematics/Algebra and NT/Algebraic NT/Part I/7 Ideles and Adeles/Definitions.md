# Definitions - Chapter VII: Ideles and Adeles

## §1. Restricted Direct Products

>[!tips] 7.1 Restricted Direct Product
>Let $\{G_v\}$ be a family of locally compact groups, and $H_v \subset G_v$ open compact subgroups defined for almost all $v$. The **restricted direct product** is the group of elements $(x_v)$ such that $x_v \in H_v$ for almost all $v$.

## §2. Adeles

>[!tips] 7.2 Adele Ring ($\mathbb{A}_k$)
>The **Adele ring** $\mathbb{A}_k$ is the restricted direct product of the completions $k_v$ with respect to the rings of integers $\mathfrak{o}_v$.
>$\mathbb{A}_k \cong \mathbb{A}_{\mathbb{Q}} \otimes_{\mathbb{Q}} k$.

## §3. Ideles

>[!tips] 7.3 Idele Group ($J_k$)
>The **Idele group** $J_k$ (often denoted $\mathbb{I}_k$) is the restricted direct product of the multiplicative groups $k_v^*$ with respect to the units $U_v$. It is the group of units of $\mathbb{A}_k$.

>[!tips] 7.3 Idele Class Group ($C_k$)
>The quotient group $C_k = J_k / k^*$, where $k^*$ is embedded diagonally into $J_k$.
