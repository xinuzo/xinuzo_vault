>[!tips] Theorem 24.1 Ring Arithmetic
>In any ring: $a0 = 0a = 0$, $a(-b) = -(ab) = (-a)b$, $(-a)(-b) = ab$.

>[!tips] Theorem 25.1 Cancellation in Integral Domains
>In an integral domain, if $a \neq 0$ and $ab = ac$, then $b = c$.

>[!tips] Theorem 25.3 Finite Integral Domains
>Every finite integral domain is a field.

>[!tips] Theorem 27.1 Characteristic of a Domain
>The characteristic of an integral domain is either 0 or a prime number $p$.
