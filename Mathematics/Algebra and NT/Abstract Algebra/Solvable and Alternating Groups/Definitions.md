>[!tips] 54.1 Normal Series
>A normal series of a group $G$ is a finite sequence $G = G_0 \supseteq G_1 \supseteq \dots \supseteq G_n = \{e\}$ where each $G_{i+1}$ is a normal subgroup of $G_i$.

>[!tips] 55.1 Alternating Group $A_n$
>The subgroup of $S_n$ consisting of all even permutations. Order is $n!/2$.

>[!tips] 55.1 Simple Group
>A group $G \neq \{e\}$ is simple if its only normal subgroups are $\{e\}$ and $G$ itself.
