>[!tips] 14.1 Order of an Element
>The order of an element $a$ in a group $G$, denoted $o(a)$, is the least positive integer $n$ such that $a^n = e$. If no such $n$ exists, $a$ has infinite order.

>[!tips] 15.1 Cyclic Subgroup, Generator
>For $a \in G$, the subgroup $\langle a \rangle = \{a^k : k \in \mathbb{Z}\}$ is called the cyclic subgroup generated by $a$. If $G = \langle a \rangle$, $G$ is a cyclic group and $a$ is a generator.

>[!tips] 15.1 Direct Product
>If $A$ and $B$ are groups, their direct product $A \times B$ is the set of pairs $(a, b)$ with component-wise operations.

>[!tips] 16.1 Cosets
>If $H$ is a subgroup of $G$, a right coset of $H$ is a subset $Ha = \{ha : h \in H\}$. A left coset is $aH = \{ah : h \in H\}$.

>[!tips] 17.1 Index
>The number of distinct right cosets of $H$ in $G$ is called the index of $H$ in $G$, denoted $[G : H]$.

>[!tips] 18.1 Isomorphism
>An isomorphism $\theta: G \to H$ is a one-to-one, onto mapping that preserves the operation: $\theta(xy) = \theta(x)\theta(y)$. If such a map exists, $G \cong H$.

>[!tips] 20.1 Cayley's Theorem
>Every group is isomorphic to a group of permutations.
