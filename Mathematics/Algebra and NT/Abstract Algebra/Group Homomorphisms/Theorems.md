>[!tips] Theorem 21.1 Properties of Homomorphisms
>If $\theta: G \to H$ is a homomorphism:
>(a) $\theta(e_G) = e_H$.
>(b) $\theta(g^{-1}) = \theta(g)^{-1}$.
>(c) $\theta(G)$ is a subgroup of $H$.

>[!tips] Theorem 21.2 Kernel is Normal
>If $\theta$ is a homomorphism, $\text{Ker } \theta$ is a normal subgroup of $G$.

>[!tips] Theorem 22.1 Quotient Group Construction
>The set of cosets $G/N$ is a group under $(Na)(Nb) = N(ab)$ if and only if $N$ is a normal subgroup.

>[!tips] Theorem 23.1 Fundamental Homomorphism Theorem
>If $\theta: G \to H$ is a homomorphism mapping $G$ onto $H$ with kernel $K$, then $G/K \cong H$. The isomorphism is given by $\phi(Kg) = \theta(g)$.
