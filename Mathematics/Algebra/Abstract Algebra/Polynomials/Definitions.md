>[!tips] 34.1 Polynomial Ring R[x]
>The set of polynomials with coefficients in a ring $R$.

>[!tips] 36.1 Irreducible Polynomial
>A nonconstant polynomial $f(x) \in F[x]$ is irreducible if it cannot be written as a product of two polynomials of lower degree.

>[!tips] 37.1 Unique Factorization Domain (UFD)
>An integral domain where every nonzero non-unit element can be factored uniquely (up to units and order) into irreducible elements.

>[!tips] 37.1 Euclidean Domain
>An integral domain allowing a Division Algorithm with a valuation function $d(a)$ (e.g., degree for polynomials, absolute value for integers).
