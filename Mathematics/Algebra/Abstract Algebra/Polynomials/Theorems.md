>[!tips] Theorem 35.1 Division Algorithm for F[x]
>If $f(x), g(x) \in F[x]$ with $g(x) \neq 0$, there exist unique $q(x), r(x)$ such that $f(x) = g(x)q(x) + r(x)$ with $r(x)=0$ or $\deg r < \deg g$.

>[!tips] Theorem 35.2 Remainder and Factor Theorems
>$f(c) = 0$ iff $(x-c)$ is a factor of $f(x)$.
