>[!tips] 38.1 Ideal
>A subring $I$ of $R$ is an ideal if $ar \in I$ and $ra \in I$ for all $a \in I, r \in R$.

>[!tips] 38.1 Principal Ideal
>An ideal generated by a single element, denoted $\langle a \rangle = \{ra : r \in R\}$ (in a commutative ring with unity).

>[!tips] 39.1 Quotient Ring
>If $I$ is an ideal of $R$, the set of cosets $R/I$ forms a ring under $(I+a) + (I+b) = I+(a+b)$ and $(I+a)(I+b) = I+(ab)$.

>[!tips] 41.1 Principal Ideal Domain (PID)
>An integral domain in which every ideal is a principal ideal.
