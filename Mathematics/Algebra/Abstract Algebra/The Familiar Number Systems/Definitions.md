>[!tips] 30.1 Field of Quotients
>Given an integral domain $D$, the field of quotients $F_D$ is a field constructed from pairs (fractions) of elements of $D$, formally $(a, b)$ with $b \neq 0$, representing $a/b$. $\mathbb{Q}$ is the field of quotients of $\mathbb{Z}$.

>[!tips] 32.1 Complex Numbers
>$\mathbb{C} = \{a + bi : a, b \in \mathbb{R}\}$ with $i^2 = -1$.

>[!tips] 33.1 Roots of Unity
>Solutions to $z^n = 1$ in $\mathbb{C}$. The primitive $n$th roots of unity generate all $n$th roots.
