>[!tips] 21.1 Homomorphism
>A mapping $\theta: G \to H$ is a homomorphism if $\theta(xy) = \theta(x)\theta(y)$ for all $x, y \in G$.

>[!tips] 21.1 Kernel
>The kernel of a homomorphism $\theta: G \to H$ is $\text{Ker } \theta = \{g \in G : \theta(g) = e_H\}$.

>[!tips] 21.1 Normal Subgroup
>A subgroup $N$ of $G$ is normal, denoted $N \triangleleft G$, if $gNg^{-1} = N$ (or $gng^{-1} \in N$) for all $g \in G, n \in N$.

>[!tips] 22.1 Quotient Group (Factor Group)
>If $N \triangleleft G$, the set of cosets $G/N$ forms a group under the operation $(Na)(Nb) = N(ab)$. This is called the quotient group of $G$ by $N$.
