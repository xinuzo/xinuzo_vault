>[!tips] Theorem 65.1 Properties of Boolean Algebras
>Boolean algebras satisfy De Morgan's Laws, Idempotent Laws, Absorption Laws, etc., similar to the algebra of sets.

>[!tips] Theorem 66.1 Finite Boolean Algebras
>Every finite Boolean algebra is isomorphic to the power set $\mathcal{P}(S)$ of some finite set $S$. Consequently, the order of any finite Boolean algebra must be a power of 2 ($2^n$).
