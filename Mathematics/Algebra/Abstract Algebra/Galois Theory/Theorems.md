>[!tips] Theorem 42.1 Simple Algebraic Extension
>If $E = F(\alpha)$ is a simple extension with $\alpha$ algebraic over $F$, then $E \cong F[x]/\langle p(x) \rangle$, where $p(x)$ is an irreducible polynomial in $F[x]$ such that $p(\alpha) = 0$.

>[!tips] Theorem 42.3 Construction of Roots
>If $F$ is a field and $p(x)$ is irreducible over $F$, then $F[x]/\langle p(x) \rangle$ is a field extension of $F$ containing a root of $p(x)$.

>[!tips] Theorem 43.1 Degree Multiplicativity
>If $F \subseteq K \subseteq E$ are fields, then $[E:F] = [E:K][K:F]$.

>[!tips] Theorem 44.1 Fundamental Theorem of Galois Theory (Preview)
>For a suitable extension $K$ of $F$ (a normal and separable extension), there is a one-to-one correspondence between the subfields of $K$ containing $F$ and the subgroups of the Galois group $\text{Gal}(K/F)$.

>[!tips] Theorem 45.1 Finite Implies Algebraic
>Every finite extension is an algebraic extension.

>[!tips] Theorem 48.1 Fundamental Theorem of Galois Theory
>Let $K$ be a finite normal separable extension of $F$ with Galois group $G = \text{Gal}(K/F)$. There is a bijection between subfields $E$ ($F \subseteq E \subseteq K$) and subgroups $H$ of $G$.
>1. $E \leftrightarrow H = \text{Gal}(K/E)$.
>2. $[K:E] = |H|$ and $[E:F] = [G:H]$.
>3. $E$ is a normal extension of $F$ iff $H$ is a normal subgroup of $G$. In this case, $\text{Gal}(E/F) \cong G/H$.

>[!tips] Theorem 49.2 Galois Criterion for Solvability
>Let $F$ be a field of characteristic 0. A polynomial $f(x) \in F[x]$ is solvable by radicals iff its Galois group (over $F$) is a solvable group.

>[!tips] Theorem 49.3 Unsolvability of the Quintic
>The general polynomial of degree $n \ge 5$ is not solvable by radicals because the symmetric group $S_n$ is not solvable for $n \ge 5$.
