>[!tips] Theorem 54.1 First Isomorphism Theorem
>If $H$ and $K$ are subgroups of $G$ with $K \triangleleft G$, then $HK$ is a subgroup, $K \triangleleft HK$, and $HK/K \cong H/(H \cap K)$.

>[!tips] Theorem 54.2 Second Isomorphism Theorem
>If $K \triangleleft H$, $H \triangleleft G$, and $K \triangleleft G$, then $(G/K) / (H/K) \cong G/H$.

>[!tips] Theorem 54.3 Solvability of Quotients
>Any homomorphic image (quotient group) of a solvable group is solvable.

>[!tips] Theorem 54.4 Solvability of Subgroups
>Every subgroup of a solvable group is solvable.

>[!tips] Theorem 55.1 Simplicity of $A_n$
>The alternating group $A_n$ is simple for all $n \ge 5$. (This is crucial for proving the insolvability of the quintic).
