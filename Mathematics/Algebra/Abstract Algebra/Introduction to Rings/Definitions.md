>[!tips] 24.1 Ring
>A ring is a set $R$ with two operations ($+, \cdot$) such that:
>1. $(R, +)$ is an Abelian group.
>2. Multiplication is associative.
>3. Distributive laws hold: $a(b+c) = ab + ac$ and $(a+b)c = ac + bc$.

>[!tips] 24.1 Commutative Ring / Unity
>A ring is commutative if $ab=ba$. It has a unity (identity) $e$ if $ea=ae=a$ for all $a$.

>[!tips] 25.1 Zero Divisor
>A nonzero element $a \in R$ is a zero divisor if there exists $b \neq 0$ such that $ab=0$ or $ba=0$.

>[!tips] 25.1 Integral Domain
>A commutative ring with unity and no zero divisors.

>[!tips] 25.1 Field
>A commutative ring with unity where every nonzero element has a multiplicative inverse.

>[!tips] 27.1 Characteristic
>The characteristic of a ring $R$ is the least positive integer $n$ such that $n \cdot a = 0$ for all $a \in R$. If no such $n$ exists, the characteristic is 0.
