>[!tips] Theorem 14.1 Cancellation and Inverses
>In a group $G$:
>(a) Left and right cancellation laws hold ($ab=ac \implies b=c$).
>(b) Equations $ax=b$ and $ya=b$ have unique solutions.
>(c) $(a^{-1})^{-1} = a$ and $(ab)^{-1} = b^{-1}a^{-1}$.

>[!tips] Theorem 15.2 Smallest Subgroup
>$\langle S \rangle$ is the unique smallest subgroup of $G$ containing the set $S$.

>[!tips] Theorem 16.1 Coset Partition
>The right cosets of a subgroup $H$ in $G$ form a partition of $G$. $a \sim b$ iff $ab^{-1} \in H$ is an equivalence relation.

>[!tips] Theorem 17.1 Lagrange's Theorem
>If $G$ is a finite group and $H$ is a subgroup, then $|G| = |H| \cdot [G : H]$.
>Corollary: The order of an element divides the order of the group.

>[!tips] Theorem 17.2 Groups of Prime Order
>Every group of prime order is cyclic.

>[!tips] Theorem 19.3 Classification of Prime Order
>If $p$ is prime, any group of order $p$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb{Z}_p$.

>[!tips] Fundamental Theorem of Finite Abelian Groups
>Every finite Abelian group is the direct product of cyclic groups of prime power order.
