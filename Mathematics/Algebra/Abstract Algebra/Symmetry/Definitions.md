>[!tips] 59.1 Symmetry Group
>The group of all isometries (distance-preserving maps) that map a figure onto itself.

>[!tips] 59.1 Dihedral Group $D_n$
>The symmetry group of a regular n-sided polygon. Contains $n$ rotations and $n$ reflections. Order $2n$.

>[!tips] 59.1 Cyclic Group $C_n$
>The symmetry group consisting only of rotational symmetries of a regular directed polygon (like a pinwheel). Order $n$.

>[!tips] 61.1 Crystallographic Restriction
>In a crystal lattice, the only possible orders for rotational symmetry are 1, 2, 3, 4, and 6.
