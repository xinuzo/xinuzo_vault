>[!tips] Theorem 59.2 Classification of Finite Plane Symmetry Groups
>A finite symmetry group of a plane figure is either a cyclic group $C_n$ or a dihedral group $D_n$.

>[!tips] Theorem 61.1 Crystallographic Restriction
>Any rotation in a 2D or 3D crystallographic point group must have order 1, 2, 3, 4, or 6. Rotations of order 5 (or $\ge 7$) are impossible in a lattice.
