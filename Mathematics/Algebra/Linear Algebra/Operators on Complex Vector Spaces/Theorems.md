>[!tips] 8.11 null spaces of powers of an operator
>Suppose $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. Then
>$$\{0\} = \text{null } T^0 \subset \text{null } T^1 \subset \dots \subset \text{null } T^k \subset \text{null } T^{k+1} \subset \dots$$

>[!tips] 8.21 decomposition of $V$
>Suppose $V$ is a complex vector space and $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. Let $\lambda_1, \dots, \lambda_m$ be the distinct eigenvalues of $T$. Then
>$$V = G(\lambda_1, T) \oplus \dots \oplus G(\lambda_m, T)$$

>[!tips] 8.23 basis of generalized eigenvectors
>Suppose $V$ is a complex vector space and $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. Then there is a basis of $V$ consisting of generalized eigenvectors of $T$.

>[!tips] 8.37 Cayley-Hamilton Theorem
>Suppose $V$ is a complex vector space and $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. Let $q$ denote the characteristic polynomial of $T$. Then $q(T) = 0$.

>[!tips] 8.49 characteristic polynomial is a multiple of minimal polynomial
>Suppose $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. Then the characteristic polynomial of $T$ is a polynomial multiple of the minimal polynomial of $T$.

>[!tips] 8.60 Jordan Form
>Suppose $V$ is a complex vector space and $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. Then there is a basis of $V$ that is a Jordan basis for $T$.
