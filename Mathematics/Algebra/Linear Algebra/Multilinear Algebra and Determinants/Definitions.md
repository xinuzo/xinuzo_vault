>[!tips] 10.3 trace of a matrix
>The **trace** of a square matrix $A$, denoted $\text{tr } A$, is defined to be the sum of the diagonal entries of $A$.

>[!tips] 10.10 trace of an operator
>Suppose $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. The **trace** of $T$, denoted $\text{tr } T$, is the sum of the eigenvalues of $T$, where each eigenvalue is repeated according to its multiplicity.

>[!tips] 10.25 determinant of an operator, $\det T$
>Suppose $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. The **determinant** of $T$, denoted $\det T$, is the product of the eigenvalues of $T$, where each eigenvalue is repeated according to its multiplicity.

>[!tips] 10.38 determinant of a matrix
>The **determinant of a matrix** $A$, denoted $\det A$, is defined to be $\det T$, where $T$ is the operator whose matrix with respect to some basis is $A$.
