>[!tips] 10.6 trace of matrix of operator does not depend on basis
>Suppose $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. Then $\text{tr } \mathcal{M}(T)$ is the same for every basis of $V$.

>[!tips] 10.14 trace of product of operators
>If $S, T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$, then $\text{tr}(ST) = \text{tr}(TS)$.

>[!tips] 10.30 invertible is equivalent to nonzero determinant
>An operator on $V$ is invertible if and only if its determinant is nonzero.

>[!tips] 10.32 determinant is multiplicative
>Suppose $S, T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. Then
>$$\det(ST) = (\det S)(\det T)$$

>[!tips] 10.42 characteristic polynomial and determinant
>Suppose $T \in \mathcal{L}(V)$. Then the characteristic polynomial of $T$ equals $\det(zI - T)$.
