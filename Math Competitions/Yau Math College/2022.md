>[!example] Problem 2
>Consider the equation $f(x) = x^3 -x-1 \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$. Let $p \neq 23$ be a prime number.
>
>(a) Prove that $f(x) \equiv 0 \pmod p$ has exactly one root in $\mathbb{F}_p$ if and only if $\left(\frac{p}{23}\right) = -1$.
>
>(b) Let $K/\mathbb{Q}$ be the Galois closure corresponding to $f$. For $p$ as in (a), determine the values of $e, f, g$ in the prime decomposition of $p\mathcal{O}_K$.
