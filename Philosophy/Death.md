Lec1 :
The nature of persons: Dualism vs Physicalism
We first have to define what is a person?
Dualism: Person is combination of mind and body
Body is a material object, mind is an immaterial object
Physicalism: Person is just a body, that is functional. 

Wrong questions: "Is there life after my death", "will I survive my death?"
right questions: "Will my soul survive after my body dies", "will I still exist after my body dies"
Will the destruction of the body destroys the soul?
